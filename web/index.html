Click the game to give it the keyboard.
Main menu: ↑/↓ (or P1 aim) pick · Enter (or fire) selects · Esc (or jump) goes to QUIT TO OS · NEW GAME starts weapon selection; Esc in a match pauses to the menu (RESUME GAME)
Match setup: F7 (or Enter on MATCH SETUP) moves the cursor into the settings · ←/→ change a value · Enter on a number types a new one (digits, then Enter; Esc cancels) · WEAPON OPTIONS: ←/→ makes a weapon Menu / Bonus / Banned, Esc closes · Esc back to the main menu · edits reach a paused match at RESUME GAME
Player 1: R/F aim · D/G move · Left Ctrl fire · hold Left Shift and tap D/G to change weapon · Left Alt jump
Player 2: ↑/↓ aim · ←/→ move · Right Ctrl fire · hold Right Alt and tap ←/→ to change weapon · Right Shift jump
Phone: tap WEAPON for the next weapon (or hold it and tap the pad ←/→ as on a keyboard; WEAPON + JUMP throws the rope)
Weapon selection first, both players: aim up/down picks a line · move left/right changes the weapon · fire on Randomize re-rolls all five · fire on DONE! when ready
Dig: hold left + right · F5 restarts a match (back to weapon selection)
URL (weapons/level/seed skip the menu): ?weapons=MISSILE,LASER,BIG%20NUKE (skips weapon selection) · &level=water_stage instead of a generated level (render_stage, water_stage, see_shadow_test, physics_fall_test) · &seed=7 fixes the seed · ?menu=1 shows the menu even with weapons/level/seed · ?demo for the scripted demo · ?touch=1 for on-screen controls
Settings are kept until you reload the page.
